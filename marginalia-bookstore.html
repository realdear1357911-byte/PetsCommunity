Our story

A shop built on marginal notes

Marginalia opened in 2011 above a print shop, with three bookcases and a coffee pot that never quite turned off. We built this site so the same shelves — and the same opinions — could reach further than the block we're on. Every book listed here has been read by someone on staff, and most still have a sticky note tucked inside.

We keep the shop small on purpose. Fewer titles, chosen carefully, beats a warehouse of everything.
